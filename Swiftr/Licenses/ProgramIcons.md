# Program icons

The icons in `Assets.xcassets/Programs` come from herdr-app's icons, under its program table's keys:

- Brand icons: Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org), CC0 1.0.
- Glyphs (shell, program, agent, search, activity…): Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC. See LICENSE-Lucide.txt.
- Agent icons: LobeHub icons (MIT) and the agents' own marks. See LICENSE-LobeHub.txt.

Two differ from herdr-app's: Claude (`claude`) and OpenAI (`codex`) come from LobeHub icons 1.102.0, MIT.

Brands belong to their owners. These licenses grant no trademark rights.
